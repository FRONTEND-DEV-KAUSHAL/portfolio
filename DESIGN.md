## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a high-energy, unapologetic, and tactile personality built specifically for creative engineers and digital craftspeople. Inspired by modern retro-web nostalgia, cyber-punk ephemera, and tactile physical interfaces, it rejects generic corporate tech minimalism in favor of structured exuberance, mechanical confidence, and visual punch.

The aesthetic philosophy centers around Neo-Brutalism:
- **Graphic Structuralism:** Stark, deep obsidian outlines (2px to 4px) anchor every interactive and structural element.
- **Physical Affordance:** Tactile push-state physics with zero-blur, solid offset shadows replicate vintage arcade machinery, mechanical keyboards, and sticker books.
- **Vibrant Saturated Accents:** Warm cyber yellow, electric lilac, and coral pink juxtaposed against quiet cream and heavy black inks.
- **Playful Pragmatism:** Layouts utilize notebook folder tabs, sticker chips, and mechanical grid alignments to convey engineering mastery paired with human personality.

## Colors

The palette relies on high-contrast chromatic interplay where ink-black boundaries partition high-voltage hues against a comforting, warm cream ground:

- **Primary (`#FFBA28`):** The signature cyber warm yellow. Applied as the primary hero background, high-priority interactive triggers, and dominant visual weight.
- **Secondary (`#C4B5FD`):** Electric pastel lavender/lilac. Used for sectional contrasts, alternating card panels, and secondary focal zones.
- **Tertiary (`#FB7185`):** High-saturation coral orange. Reserved for badges, micro-highlights, attention flags, and active state alerts.
- **Neutral (`#111827`):** Deep obsidian ink. Used for typography, thick component borders, hard offset shadows, and structural silhouettes.
- **Canvas (`#FAF8F5` & `#ECEBE0`):** Vintage eggshell/cream background shades that soften high-contrast outlines while retaining daylight luminosity.

## Typography

Typography balances bold, brutal display weights with razor-sharp technical legibility:

- **Headlines (Syne):** Set in Extra-Bold and Bold weights. The geometric, tightly kerned glyphs deliver punchy visual presence reminiscent of physical print posters and heavy graphic design zines.
- **Body Copy (Space Grotesk):** Provides high readability with subtle retro-futuristic grotesque quirks. Maintains structural cohesion with the heavy display typography without causing visual fatigue.
- **Labels & Metas (Space Mono):** Used for code snippets, project status stamps, dates, and architectural tags, adding an authentic developer-first terminal texture.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in structured, hard-edged modular sections:

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views utilize a 12-column responsive layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` outer page margins. Mobile layouts collapse to a 4-column flow with `1rem` margins and gutters.
- **Section Enclosures:** Major sections (e.g., About, Projects, Skills) are wrapped in monolithic full-width or bounded containers framed with `3px` solid obsidian borders.
- **Rhythm & Density:** Generous internal card padding (`1.5rem` to `2.5rem`) balances compact UI controls (`space-xs` and `space-sm`), ensuring components never feel cramped despite their heavy black stroke profiles.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately eschews realistic lighting, blurred box-shadows, and translucent backdrop filters. Elevation is purely tactile, directional, and geometric:

- **Level 1 (Interactive Base / Badges):** `3px 3px 0px #111827`. Used for tag chips, small buttons, and skill cards.
- **Level 2 (Cards / Primary Components):** `5px 5px 0px #111827`. Used for project panels, tabbed windows, and highlighted showcase blocks.
- **Level 3 (Modals / Floating Heroes):** `8px 8px 0px #111827`. Used for key visual anchors and hero badges.
- **Interaction Physics:** Hovering pushes elements up and left (`transform: translate(-2px, -2px)`) while expanding the shadow. Pressing down (`:active`) triggers the mechanical click: `transform: translate(3px, 3px)` with shadow reducing to `0px 0px 0px #111827` or `1px 1px 0px #111827`.

## Shapes

The system adopts a controlled "Soft Brutalism" geometry:
- **Base Elements & Cards:** Radii default to `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.5rem` (8px), preventing razor-sharp discomfort while strictly avoiding soft bubble aesthetics.
- **Tabbed Folders & Section Tags:** Header badges feature flat bottom edges with rounded top corners (`0.5rem 0.5rem 0 0`) to simulate physical index cards and manila desktop folders.
- **Pills & Status Micro-Badges:** Rounded to `9999px` to create a deliberate contrast against the blocky container frames.
- **Borders:** All structural boundaries use explicit `2px` to `4px` solid `#111827` borders.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#FFBA28`, border `3px solid #111827`, text `#111827`, font `Space Grotesk` weight 700. Hard shadow `4px 4px 0px #111827`.
- **Secondary:** Background `#FFFFFF` or `#C4B5FD`, border `3px solid #111827`. Hard shadow `4px 4px 0px #111827`.
- **States:** On hover, translate `-2px, -2px` with shadow expanded to `6px 6px 0px #111827`. On active, translate `3px, 3px` with shadow `1px 1px 0px #111827`.

### Cards & Project Showcases
- **Container:** Outer border `3px solid #111827`, background `#FFBA28` or `#C4B5FD`, shadow `6px 6px 0px #111827`.
- **Inner Frame:** Embedded media and screenshots are framed within an inner `2px` or `3px` solid black border, sitting on `#FFFFFF` or `#FAF8F5`.
- **Tab Header:** Top tab element attached directly to card boundaries, labeled with icons and `Space Mono` typography.

### Skill Badges & Tech Stack Grid
- **Geometry:** Square or proportional rectangles, `3px solid #111827` border, rounded `4px`.
- **Palette:** Bright yellow `#FFBA28` or lilac `#C4B5FD` surface, black glyph/icon centered, bold label underneath.
- **Shadow:** `4px 4px 0px #111827`.

### Pills & Tags
- **Structure:** Rounded `9999px`, border `2px solid #111827`, background `#FB7185` or `#C4B5FD`.
- **Shadow:** `2px 2px 0px #111827`. Text styled with `Space Mono` bold.

### Form Inputs
- **Field:** Background `#FAF8F5`, border `3px solid #111827`, shadow `4px 4px 0px #111827`.
- **Focus State:** Background `#FFFFFF`, shadow shifts to `6px 6px 0px #FFBA28` or retains `#111827` outline with inner accent glow.
- **Labels:** Bold `Space Grotesk` positioned directly above the input with `space-xs` gap.

### Section Header Badges
- **Folder Tab Style:** Protruding tab attached to section dividers with an icon, bold title, and `3px` continuous border, mimicking retro desktop file systems.
